## AR Specific Settings
- Use the hardware or software hotkey on your glasses to switch to Full-SbS mode
- Run Shader Glass
- Set `Output - Aspect Ratio Correction` to `x0.5 (double wide)`
- Under `Processing` click `Set as default profile`
- Close Shader Glass
